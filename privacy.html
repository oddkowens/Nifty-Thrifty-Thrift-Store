Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2025

Nifty Thrifty Thrift Shop ("we", "us", or "our") operates the website at niftythriftyilm.com. This page explains what information is collected when you visit our site and how it is used. We keep this simple because our site is simple — we don't sell anything online, we don't run accounts, and we don't collect your personal information directly.

Information we collect

We do not collect your name, email address, or any personal information through this website directly. The only data collected comes from two third-party services we use:

  • Google Analytics — collects anonymized information about how visitors use the site, including pages visited, time spent, general geographic location (city/region level), device type, and how you arrived at the site (e.g. from a search engine or social media). This uses cookies.
  • Cloudflare — provides security and performance services for our website. Cloudflare may collect certain technical data such as IP addresses and browser information as part of its service.

Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device by your browser. Google Analytics uses cookies to distinguish visitors and track sessions. Cloudflare uses cookies for security purposes.

You can disable cookies at any time through your browser settings. Doing so will not affect your ability to use this website — you'll still be able to view all of our content.

How we use this information

The analytics data we receive helps us understand how many people visit the site, which information is most useful, and whether the site is performing well on different devices. We use this only to improve the website experience.

We do not sell, rent, or share any data with third parties for marketing purposes.

Third-party links

Our site contains links to Google Maps, Facebook, and Instagram. These services have their own privacy policies, which we encourage you to review. We are not responsible for the practices of third-party sites.

Children's privacy

This website is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect any information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date at the top. Continued use of the site after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
